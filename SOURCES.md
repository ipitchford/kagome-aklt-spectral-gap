# Sources and bounded prior-art comparison

Checked 30 September 2026 at publication intake. This is retrospective, not a backdated discovery search. Searches covered the exact model, kagome AKLT spectral gap, decorated versus undecorated lattices, projector compression, weighted finite-size criteria and spectral-gap hierarchies. No identical complete theorem was identified in this bounded search; that is not worldwide novelty or priority clearance.

* Affleck, Kennedy, Lieb and Tasaki (1988), Valence bond ground states in isotropic quantum antiferromagnets. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01218021). Model and frustration-free background, not a certificate for this kagome result.
* Knabe (1988), Energy gaps and elementary excitations for certain VBS-quantum antiferromagnets. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01019721). Finite-size criterion antecedent; the specific cover-counting argument used here is written in the manuscript.
* Pomata and Wei (2020), Demonstrating the AKLT spectral gap on 2D degree-3 lattices. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.01410), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.124.177203). Ground-projector compression and weighted-cover context; not an earlier spin-2 undecorated-kagome certificate.
* Guo, Pomata and Wei (2021), Nonzero spectral gap in several uniformly spin-2 and hybrid-spin AKLT models. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.03137), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.3.013255). Section IV.A discusses the natural kagome cover: the computed pair overlap exceeds 1/6, so that pair-only route does not prove the gap. The present candidate uses a grouped three-block argument, not a reinterpretation of that failed threshold as success.
* Lemm, Sandvik and Wang (2020), Existence of a spectral gap in the Affleck–Kennedy–Lieb–Tasaki model on the hexagonal lattice. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1910.11810), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.124.177204). Weighted finite-size criterion combined with DMRG for the hexagonal spin-3/2 model; distinguishes the analytic criterion from the numerical estimate. Relevant methodological antecedent, different model and computational acceptance boundary.
* Rai et al. (2026), A hierarchy of spectral gap certificates for frustration-free spin systems. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.03680v3), [Published record](https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2026-04-13-2065/), DOI 10.22331/q-2026-04-13-2065. Local positive decompositions and a hierarchy containing finite-size bounds. The candidate's finite-sector grouping is a specialised construction; it makes no claim to replace or outperform that hierarchy.
* Rump (2011), Verified bounds for singular values, in particular for the spectral norm of a matrix and its inverse. [Full text](https://www.tuhh.de/ti3/paper/rump/Ru10a.pdf), DOI 10.1007/s10543-010-0294-0. Shifted approximate Cholesky plus rigorously bounded residual is the relevant positivity-verification idea; completion of Cholesky alone is not certification.
* Jackson, Nachtergaele and Young (2026), Local topological quantum order and spectral gap stability of the AKLT models on the hexagonal and Lieb lattices. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12184v3). Abstract-level scope checked for contextual nonclaims only; no theorem from this preprint is a dependency of the kagome proof, and its lattice-specific stability conclusions are not transferred here.

Publicly checkable references support the cited comparisons, not the correctness of the new theorem. See the paper for the complete proof obligations and ASSURANCE.md for the implementation contract.
